InkBased Design

A collection of illustrations produced since 1999, using ink, graphite pencil and other selected traditional medium. Technology from the cloud direct to our artificially intelligent screen devices is casting shadows over the old art techniques ... this is the fired furnace for crafting with the techniques of an older world, soon to be forgotten by future generations. However ... every time one of these relics light up the pixels or given the Lazeranian second breath of life, rooted to the other ancient craft loved by man — in the presentation and drinking of good wine, joy wells up from experiencing their combined quality and singularity in existance.
